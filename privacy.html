Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VIRAL CLIPPER AI handles information when users use the application.

2. Information We Process

The application may process account authorization information, platform data, video and audio content, and technical information necessary to provide its features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to discover gaming livestreams, analyze audio, identify highlights, create video clips, and support user-authorized publishing.

4. Video and Audio

Video and audio may be processed to identify interesting moments and generate short-form clips. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to use third-party content.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may connect to services such as TikTok, YouTube, and Twitch. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect account credentials, authorization tokens, and other information processed by the application.

7. Data Retention

Information should only be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's features or meet applicable legal requirements.

8. User Choices

Users control whether generated clips are approved for publishing. Users may disconnect authorized accounts.

9. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests can be directed to the application developer using the contact information provided with the application.
